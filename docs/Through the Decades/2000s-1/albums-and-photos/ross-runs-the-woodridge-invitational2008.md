We were at the Woodridge Invitational this morning. The course is very challenging — lots of big, steep hills.

Kenston boys cross country had an outstanding finish– Second Place out of approx. twenty teams! Very Exciting!!!! Our favorite runner placed third overall, with a two mile time of 11:59.

FYI The next meet is this Wednesday, at Frohring Meadows off Savage Road, 4PM. I understand that the road is closed, and there is parking available at the Armory on Washington Street. (We’ll be checking the KMS Athletics Website for more information.) Grace 09-27-08
