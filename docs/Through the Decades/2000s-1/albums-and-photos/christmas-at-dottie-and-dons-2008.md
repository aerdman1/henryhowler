Christmas at D & D’s this year was extra special. Not just because we had visitors from afar like the Billiar family, Pat, Lori, Robyn, Polly and Adam but because everybody came prepared to put personal items into the what will be the New Time Capsule to be buried at the shed. It was great to hear predictions of what our lives will be like in 15 years. Pictures, cards, receipts, newspaper articles, lots of Obama paraphernalia and written predictions were collected. Jim and Chip have a plan for a bigger and better and water resistant Time Capsule to be buried soon. There is still time to add stuff…..send to Dottie or Jim.

December 26, 2008 Linda
