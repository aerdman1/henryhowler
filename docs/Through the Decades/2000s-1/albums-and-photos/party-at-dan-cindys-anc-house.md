Dan wrote on 10-27-08

We had a great party tonight (Sunday) celebrating all of Cindy’s uncles and other family in from St. Paul Island for AFN (Alaska Federation of Natives).  We had burgers and brats and lots of salads.  Pat and I we the un denied champions of corn hole until near the end of the night.  After Pat and I went back to 13 3 times, the other team edged ahead for the win.  They were thrilled to have bragging rights that they beat Pat and I.

Robin H was here too.  We all had a great visit.  Must of been a dozen or more kids between the age of 3 and 10.  They were like a pack of dogs but funner and a little less bitey.

People seem to have a very nice time. Hope you all had a nice weekend.

love Dan

 08 Oct October 27, 2008 Linda
