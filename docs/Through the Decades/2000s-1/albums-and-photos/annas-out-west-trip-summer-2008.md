Anna was a counselor on the famous Kenston High School Out West Trip this summer. She sent me a photo journal of beautiful pictures that she took along the way.

“Overall, the trip was great! I really enjoyed visiting all the different national parks, caves, and monuments.  Even though it you’d think the parks are similar, each one had its one neat feature. With having only 2 showers on the 3.5-week trip, it was nice to be home again … and to taking showers daily!”

August 10, 2008
