Chip and I had a weekend visit in Denver with Adam. We got to stay at the house he has just bought and got to meet his girlfriend Ashley. We saw that sights around the city, went to the top of the Wells Fargo Bank building where Adam works for Wells Fargo in their Private Banking offices. We also had a Peace Corps reunion of sorts, getting together with our PC Lesotho friends Rich & Maureen Harter and Patricia & Britt Foley-Hinen.

We really enjoyed Denver and Adam has made a happy, comfortable life there. Linda
