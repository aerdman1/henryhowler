This article about the long standing relationship between Hiram College and the Henry family is fun to read. According to Heidi, Fred is wearing a traditional "Freshman Beanie Cap".  I found this info on the web:

> In the 1930s, college freshman caps, often called beanies or dinks, were mandatory, conspicuous hats (usually green or black) worn by male freshmen to identify them as "newbies" for upperclassmen's hazing and initiation rituals, symbolizing their novice status until an end-of-year event like a bonfire or flag day
