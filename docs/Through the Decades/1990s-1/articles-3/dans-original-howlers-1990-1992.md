Back in the day, before the World Wide Web, Dan Henry had an idea.  He wanted all of the far flung family to have a way to keep up-to-date on what everyone was doing.  He wrote, published and mailed a family newsletter (using snail mail!) to each of us.  He called and hounded family members to write and send him articles and photos –  for a couple years he was relentless.  Life though, got busy for Dan and he stopped publishing in 1992. We are very grateful to Dan for all of his efforts.  What a treasure these newsletters are! We are also grateful to Mom and Grace for saving each and every one which I have scanned and made into PDF’s.   Linda
