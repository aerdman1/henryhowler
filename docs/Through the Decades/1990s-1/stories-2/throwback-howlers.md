Just for fun….and because I didn’t have a lot of material this month, I scanned in another vintage Henry Howler from May 1991. This is a 12 page classic! Read about Patrick apologizing for peeing on video tape (it was an instance of the camera taping when he thought it was turned off), an article about Rick and Laura’s new Invisible Fence company, a bear hunt story by Dan and “Star Hopper” a story , written by Zach (about age 9).

I promise it’s all good stuff.
