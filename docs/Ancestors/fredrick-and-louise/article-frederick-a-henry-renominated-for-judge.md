Judge Henry is renominated for Circuit Court Judge. In his acceptance speech he “declared emphatically for judges who declare the law as it is rather than as they think it ought to be.  Any other course is tyranny.” he said.
Also Judge Henry said the crying evil of the day in the courts is the delay in the adjudication of cases.
