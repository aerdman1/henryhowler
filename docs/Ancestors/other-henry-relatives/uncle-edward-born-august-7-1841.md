United States Express Guard to Government Money.
Edward Everett Henry, Age 64. (Born 7 Aug. 1841)
Hours of Firing at Practice, 10 A.M. to 12 M.
Trips to United States Treasury, Washington, D.C.
To United States Mint, Philadelphia, Pa.
Mem-orandums. Generally three Wrecks a year.
No sleeping or Leaving of Money Safes or Boxes, until Delivery is made. Sub-Treasury for the Western States, Chicago.
Gold Bullion from Denver and Pueblo Smelters to U.S. Mint Philadelphia, Month of June 1903, Nearly Seven Millions of Gold.
Arms, Colt's Automatic 7-Shooter, Maxim Attachment.
Winchester, Magazine, 18 shots in all. Basket with rations, and Heavy Over-coat.
