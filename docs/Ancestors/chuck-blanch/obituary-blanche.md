BLANCHE HAWLEY HENRY
Blanche Hawley Henry, 98, of Bainbridge Township, died Jan. 10 at St. Luke's Hospital following a brief illness.
Life and Education
Born in West Rupert, Vt., on Sept. 11, 1892.
1918 graduate of Hiram College.
Taught school for a few years in Vermont.
Hiram resident for 20 years.
Bainbridge resident for 50 years.
Family
Member of Valley Presbyterian Church.
Met her husband, Charles A. Henry, at college and married in 1918 upon graduation. He preceded her in death in 1982.
Survived by sons: John W. and Donald G. Henry.
Survived by 18 grandchildren: Chris Martin, Rick, Scott, Robyn, Tim, Michael, George, Jim, Chip, Pat, Dan, Polly, Holly and Cori Henry, K.C. Bergman, Heidi Cregar, Ellen Morrison and Dodie Lee.
Survived by 30 great-grandchildren.
Preceded in death by a son, Frederick Henry, two months prior.
Services and Memorials
Services were held Jan. 14 at Valley Presbyterian Church. The family suggests memorial contributions to the Charles A. Henry Scholarship Fund, Hiram College, Hiram 44234.
