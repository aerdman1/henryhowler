Seems that while in the woods he ran across a Red Fox that I guess he thought would make a nice hat. After shooting the fox he decided the best way to carry it back to camp was to drape it over his shoulders.

After proudly returning to camp and showing off his catch, he shortly discovered to his extreme discomfort that all the cloths he was wearing were totally infested with fleas.
