One day, my brother Keith was sitting around bored and decided it would be a good idea to mess with our friend Joe T.

Keith walked out from “the Cabin” where he new Joe’s hunting camera was set up. He stripped down bare naked except his boots and walking stick and strolled right up past the camera. As he walked past the camera did its job and fired off a bunch of rapid shots.

A few days later when Joe was reviewing the tapes he discovered a lot  more than just some passing critters.\
