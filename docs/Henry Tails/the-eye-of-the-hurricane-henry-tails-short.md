Another time was a fishing trip in Canada. All the kids were in one cabin.\
A pretty nasty thunderstorm had rolled through at night. I can’t recall if we were sleeping or if we were playing cards or something.
Anyways, Dan walks through the rain over to our cabin and starts pounding on the door and frantically yells “you guys got to get out of here!!! We’re in the eye of the hurricane!!!!
Go to the lodge NOW and take cover!!!!
So we panicked and ran to the lodge in this downpour.
We knocked on the door to be let in and the lodge owner said ” I think someone is messing with you guys
The Eye of The Hurricane.
-Justin Henry-
