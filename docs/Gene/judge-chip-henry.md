This is a collection of photos and articles about Chip’s career as Geauga County Juvenile/Probate Judge.  He was appointed in April, 1993 by Governor George Voinovich to succeed the late Judge Frank G. Lavrich. He was re-elected numerous times and served until his death, May 23, 2011.
