Chip and I recently visited Adam who is currently working the harvest or “crush” season at Selby Winery in Healdsburg, California which is on the beautiful Russian River in the Sonoma Valley. He has been working and learning every aspect of the wine industry and is even starting to put his Miami Marketing degree to good use by working with distributors for Selby Wine and a sideline wine called Monkey Business Wine.

Adam works 7 days a week, just like everybody does during crush, and gets around town on the skateboard he built himself before he left Ohio in July. He has put a lot of milage on that thing and has saved a lot on gas! He still plans on moving back to Denver before Christmas but I bet he will forever be California Dream’n.
