Polly and John are visiting and they planned an “Evening at the Lee Lake House”. John cooked massive amounts of barbequed ribs and everybody else brought side dishes.  Dodie and Pete’s house and yard were perfect.  The lake had some waves that were fun to “surf” with the kayaks.  Holly, Jerry, Don, Dottie, Barbie, Ellen, Mom, Dodie, Pete, Polly, John, Grace, Jim, Erin, Anna, Ross, Chip, Mike, me and 7 dogs were all there.
