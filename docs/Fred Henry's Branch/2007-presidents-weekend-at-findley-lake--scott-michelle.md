We had four feet of snow and some very small wind-chill factors on Monday, but Saturday and Sunday, it was warm enough to get the twins daily lessons and learn them how to ski!!!

Sounds good but the problem was at $220, the lessons only lasted an hour a day… so guess who became ski instructors! In spite of that, once the bunny hill was no longer a challenge, the two of them went all the way down Peak N Peak’s full range numerous times and Mighty McKinley even did it without falling down! Macho Milo was also fantastic and by the end of the second day, they could both stop on a quarter and turn on dime! Check out these pics! Not bad for 4 year olds! Huh!!!

February 28, 2007 Linda
