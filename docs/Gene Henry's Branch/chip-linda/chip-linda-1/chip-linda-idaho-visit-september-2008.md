Chip and I visited my family in Idaho for a week in September. We stayed with my sister Julie Randell and her husband Robert where they run a building contracting business. Julie is also a wildlife rehabilitator and while we were there she was rehabing a Golden Eagle and a tiny hummingbird! We spent a number of days riding motorcycles up to the Sawtooth Mountains with some of our family members and friends. I can’t say it was a relaxing vacation, we stuffed as many activities into a week as we possibly could and had a great time. Linda
