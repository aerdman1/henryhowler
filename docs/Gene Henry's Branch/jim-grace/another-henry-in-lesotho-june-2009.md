Anna had an amazing trip to Lesotho, southern Africa (yes, land of Chip and Linda!) with a group from college. Here are a few pictures and a very short summary from her:

I went to Lesotho for a month with Wittenberg doing service projects (building playgrounds, painting school buildings/orphanage rooms, fixing up community centers, building house and chicken coops…as well as spending time with kids/orphans who don’t get that much attention).  After working for three weeks, our group drove to Krugar National Park to see all of the animals!  It was incredibly  memorable and so cool to be able to see their different culture and way of living!!

Love, Anna
