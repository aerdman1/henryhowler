CHC stands for Cleveland Hiking Club.  Mom got the recipe from someone in the CHC Chili Cookoff Contest.  This was the 2nd place winner but is so easy, good and a little different so she got the recipe.

CHC Chicken Chili

3 chicken breasts – cooked and shredded

3 cans of white beans, either Great Northern or Navy

3 cups shredded cheese

1 can of chopped green chilies

1 jar of your favorite salsa

Combine everything in a slow cooker ’til cheese is melted and it is hot.  That’s it!
