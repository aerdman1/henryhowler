There is an interesting cemetery on Brewster Rd. that has old Henry tombstones.  Chip and I visited and recorded the Henry information and photos on March 24, 2007 – see Southwest Burying Ground.

In September of 2015, a group of Gene Henry’s visited the Southwest Burying Ground again with Joan Demirjian who owns the adjacent property.  She has studied the headstones there and knows a lot.  She was a very good guide and we enjoyed the fall day with her.  She also has a sugarhouse and a rope swing out over the river.
