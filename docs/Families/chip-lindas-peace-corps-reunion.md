Well, we did it. We finally got it together and had a wonderful reunion.

In August, Chip and I went to a reunion of our Peace Corps Lesotho 1980 to 1982 group. The last time we were all together was 1987 at the same place, Estes Park, Colorado. It was a warm and fuzzy weekend of being all together again, though we were missing some folks.

A big plus was a number of our kids came, including Adam! We had such a great time we have vowed to do it again, MUCH more often!
