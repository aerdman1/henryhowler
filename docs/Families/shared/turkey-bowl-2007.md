The annual Thanksgiving morning Henry Family Turkey Bowl Football game took place even though the weather was cold and soggy. Team Captains Janet and Bobbi choose up sides and the game began. Scott and Milo were formidable as were Henry and Mateo. At the point when I left, the score was tied and no one was bleeding. All had a great time. Linda
