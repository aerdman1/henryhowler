Today was Polly and John’s last day of the Ohio vacation.  Tomorrow they will pack up their their car and head back to Florida.  So, for one last hurrah, Polly planned a hike and hotdog picnic at the Caves.  Hikers were Mom, Polly, John, Dodie, Uncle Don, Jim, Holly, Jerry, Janna (Jerry’s twin sister) and her two boys, Casey and Trevor, Chip and me.  It was very hot and humid but a good time.  Trevor, Casey, Jerry and Jim went through the caves looking for bears. Linda
