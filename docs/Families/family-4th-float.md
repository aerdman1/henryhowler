This has been a hot and very busy 4th of July weekend here in O-H-I-O!

I hope we hear what everybody did to celebrate the 4th in Colorado, India, Honduras, New Hampshire, Ireland (Kris!), Alaska, Florida, Out West Somewhere (Ross!) and all the many places we are scattered in between.  Chip and I attended a wedding, neighborhood picnic, fishing, kayaking with friends, airport run to send Kimber on her way to Honduras but the weekend topper was a family kayak trip yesterday.

Cori and girls are in town for a few days passing thru from Ireland.  Kris will arrive soon and then they will all travel back together to their Mass. home. Yesterday Cori organized a picnic/kayak flotilla on the Cuyahoga.  Kayakers/canoers were, Cori, Lucy, Simone, Holly, Chris, Liz, Don, George, Sharon, JB, Kevin, Janna (Jerry’s twin sister), Janna’s two boys Trevor & Casey, and me (Linda).  Helping and attending the picnic was Jeanne, Barbie, Dottie, Ellen and of course Chip.

Happy Independance Day everyone!
