Liz says…. “I’m staying in a room at the orphanage El Hogar Suyapa with the 21 children from 3 months to 7 years. There´s a staff of rotating nannies on shift and I’m trying very hard to remember everyone’s names although I´ve met at least 60 children and adults in the last two days! The children at the Hogar are very sweet and eager to play and learn. They come from a variety of situations including being found in the garbage, or being abandoned at the nutrition center.”
