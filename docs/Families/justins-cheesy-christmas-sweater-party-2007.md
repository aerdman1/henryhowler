Justin threw his Second Annual Cheesy Christmas Sweater Party at his lovely Lyndhurst home this past December.  Justin, ever the host, even painted his dining red just before the party in order to add to the festive ambiance.  Food was provided by anyone who was willing to contribute to the pot-luck hors d’ouvres buffet, which included everything from Jello shots to shot venison.  In this second year, competition for the best outfit / worst sweater combo, was fierce.  An unnamed source confided that some people actually passed up two or three thrift stores on their way to the mall to pay full price for “totally cheesed-out attire” in hopes of winning the elusive prize awarded to the cheesiest, tackiest, but still classiest party goer.  You don’t know the winner.  Heck,  turns out no one knew the winner.  We eventually figured that out after he left, but boy was he cheesy lookin’! Andy, 01-08
