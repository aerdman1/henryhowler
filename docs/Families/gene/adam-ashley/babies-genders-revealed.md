Saturday evening (Jan. 21, 2017), Ashley and Adam hosted a joint Gender Reveal Party.  Since Ashley and Kim are due one day apart the babies will share many events…starting with their Gender Reveal.  Kim and Andrew had their ultrasound 10 days ago and could not wait to find out so they already knew what they are having but hadn’t disclosed it to the rest of us.  Ashley and Adam had taken their ultrasound results, without looking, directly to the bakery in Chagrin to have their cake ordered.
