The Gene Henry’s rented two condo’s near Vail and had a week of Colorado style fun together. Lots of snow and cold plus a hot tub and lots of friends visiting. It was a blast!

The evening before the wedding we held a rehearsal dinner at a stylish restaurant in town and also rented the Colorado Ski Museum for party to bring the Rosenthal and Henry families together.  It was a beautiful and fun evening.
