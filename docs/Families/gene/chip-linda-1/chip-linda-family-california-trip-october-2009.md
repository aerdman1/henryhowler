I have been planning my family reunion for about a year and we finally got it together. Chip and I went out a week early and met up with my sister Julie and her husband Rob. The four us visited our brother Doug in the Sonoma valley and then traveled a large loop throughout northern Calif. Adam and Kim join us about half way thru and the six of us visited Yosemite NP and the small town of Lee Vining and Mono Lake where my grandparents use to take me as a kid. Then we joined up with some of my extended family in Lake Tahoe. We were celebrating my great Aunt and Uncle’s 90th birthdays and 67th wedding anniversary! They are still very sharp and fun to be with and we all had a great time. Linda
