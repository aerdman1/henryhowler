I have an electric meat grinder that I find very useful for this recipe.

you need: 1 part beef burger 2 parts moose burger (total meat equal to the size of three man fists) italian seasoning (half a shot glass) garlic (5 cloves) Eggs (6) two eggs for every fist of meat I like dry onion flakes (fresh would be OK cut very fine) (about half a shot glass) dry bread that has been sitting up on top of the refrigerator. (a lot – about 8 slices) Mushrooms (lots one man fist) its true – mushrooms make your balls soft and moist) black pepper (you know what you like) seasoning salt (to taste)

Dan’s Saucy Balls: Make a meatless spaghetti sauce.  Now you have spaghetti and meat balls or meatball subs

Dan’s Salty Balls: sprinkle with seasoning salt – put a toothpick in each and you have party finger foods

Dan’s Sour Balls: topped with sweat and sour sauce – put a toothpick in each and you have party finger foods
