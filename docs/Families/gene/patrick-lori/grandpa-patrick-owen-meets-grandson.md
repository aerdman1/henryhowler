Grandpa Patrick Owen finally meets grandson Owen Henry. Look out, world!

Robyn is in town to spend time with her grandma. I’m going to do my best to nanny this boy. He’s about 6 weeks old so is still pretty mommy dependant. We’ll see how this goes.

Dan, Cindy and the ‘O’ boy!\* Alivia’s eskimo name is ‘Qaqiqsinauraq’ (phonetically: Kuk eek see nyour ik, AKA Kukky by grandpa). Owen’s eskimo name is ‘Maniilaq’ (AKA Lukky by grandpa). So we have Kukky and Lukky. Here is Lukky with Grand Uncle Dan\*!

Owen was resting on my chest from innings 5 through 7 of the Chicago at Cleveland game on 4/2\*…Not sure how the game ended but we were down 3-6 when Owen started needing attention. thank you Owen…looks like a long season with Cleveland Indians.
