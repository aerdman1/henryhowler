From Polly – Hey big family!  Yes, I’ve got pat down here now.  I’m at work, and looks like he’s awake at home.  He’s going to take my truck and a kayak down to Clam Pass (just a bit past Little-Big Mike’s wedding church – where that street ends is a great place to put-in).  He plans to kayak to a beach, and chill (or bake).  We’ve had beautiful weather, although he experienced Naples at its coldest last week.  Yesterday we rode with a big group from Harley up to Sanibel and Captiva.  I’m going to try to attach some pictures.  After school, pat and I will drive to the other coast, spend the night at my John’s, and head to the miami airport early in the morning.

Back to work.  Lots of love, and HAPPY BIRTHDAY SCOTT!!!!!
