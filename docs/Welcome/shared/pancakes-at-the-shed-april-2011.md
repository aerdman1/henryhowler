On Saturday, Aunt Dottie realized that Sunday would bring record warmth to cold hardened NE Ohio so she set everything in motion for an impromptu pancake brunch at the Shed.  Folks filtered in and out throughout the afternoon and the weather did not disappoint.  Jackets and sweaters got tossed aside as Dottie whipped up pancakes as fast as Chip could cook them in Gene’s big skillet.
