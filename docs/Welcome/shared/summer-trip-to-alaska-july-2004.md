Chip, Linda, Adam and Kim went to Anchorage where the group split into 3 sub groups – Guys all went to Kodiak to fish, Cindy and Linda went to visit Cindy’s family on St. Paul Island and Lori and Kim went to Kotzebue to visit Lori’s family for 4 days. Then we all came together and traveled to Talkeetna to see Robin Henry, the Cregars, and Andy Bergman. We continued to travel to Denali, looped around to Valdez then home to Anchorage where Jim had arrived to work at Answer Camp. A wonderful time to be together in Alaska!
