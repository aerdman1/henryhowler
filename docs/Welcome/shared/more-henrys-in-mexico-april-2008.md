It was the kids Spring Break so we took them to Playa Del Carmen Mexico… (thanks to American Express Air Points!!!) I learned again that “4 kids” and “vacation” still do not belong in the same sentance, regardless of ages. Also, Michelle pointed out, and I certainly agreed, that “All Inclusive Beach Resort” is an oxymoron because there’s no way to feel comfortable in your bathing suit when you’re eating and drinking as much as possible! The food was fantastic and the kids had a ball… that was especially true for our only teenager, Kyle! It was his vacation of a lifetime because he only had to “report in” to us two twice a day for 5 minutes at a time… when he needed money and suntan lotion… the rest of the time he was with people he cared about! Just A Fun Time with no educational value what so ever… Scott
