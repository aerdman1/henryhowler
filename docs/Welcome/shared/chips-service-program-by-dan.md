This is a poem from the program for Chip’s funeral service.  A small version was also made that was given out at the Burr Funeral Home during visitation hours.  ServiceCard PDF

Chip we miss you… Your whole family clan. Some how we will manage, The best that we can.

Master we miss you (woof)… To walk by your side. To ride in the kayak. With you to abide.

Chip we miss you… We brothers three. What of adventures, That were meant to be?

Chip we miss you… Your sisters two. Who’ll send the emails? Who’ll be the glue?

Dad we miss you… Who’ll give me away? Who’ll cosign the mortgage? Who turned the sky gray?

Chip I miss you… As a mother will A hole in my heart That will never fill.

My love I will miss you… But we won’t be apart. You’ll always be with me, You are my heart.
