Where I come from, Idaho, bull riding is a sport, so that is why this party is appearing on the Sports Page. Alex turned 21 and Katie turned 24 in style at Mustang Salliz, a rodeo bar in Streetsboro. Many family showed up to eat cake and celebrate but Andy, Erin, Bobbi and Jim provided the entertainment when they rode the mechanical bull. If anyone has other pictures of any other riders please forward them to me so they can be added here. Aunt Linda
