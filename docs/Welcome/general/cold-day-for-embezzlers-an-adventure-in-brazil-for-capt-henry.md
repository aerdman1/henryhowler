I have captured just three of the dozens of newspaper accounts that were written when Capt. Henry returned in 1893 from Brazil with not just the white-collar criminal that he had set out to arrest but two! He got seriously lucky and captured a second one. What a story! These newspaper clippings tell the story better and with more flair than the modest Capt. Henry’s account in the book.
