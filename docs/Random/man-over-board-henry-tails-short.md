We were up at Lady Evelyn in Canada.. We were in some old tin canoes. Gene wanted an Adult beverage..I had a beer in my canoe and I said “here” and I thru one to him.
My throw was not quite far enough.  At that moment Gene had a cigar in his mouth and a hat on.. He reached out for the tossed beer and the canoe tipped over… .Gene went under water, and wasn’t coming back up, i only saw his hat floating on the surface.
Just as I stared to worry, Gene came up underneath his hat with a cigar in his mouth and puffed on his cigar, smoke,  but no beer…
-Don Henry-

July 10, 2017 Brian Lee
