A deeply evocative story of ambition and betrayal, The Paris Wife captures the love affair between two unforgettable people: Ernest Hemingway and his wife Hadley. \~ Amazon

I got this book out of my neighborhood “little library” which is a box in a yard on S. Main St. just around the corner from me. I had low expectations but ended up really enjoyed it.  It’s my favorite kind of book, historical novel.  I’ve always been interested in Hemingway and this book gives you a picture of his early life before he becomes famous.

I highly recommend The Paris Wife!  Linda
