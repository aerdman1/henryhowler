I have scanned another howling issue of a Vintage Henry Howler (April 1991). Read articles written about Jeanne and Gene’s trip to Alaska and a sports article by Polly “The First Bout of Tennis: The Fire Is Started” .

A real piece of Henry History is a story about Gene boxing in a Golden Gloves match against “Little Bow Jackson” with Uncle Fred as his “manager”. It is truly an amazing and funny story and I am grateful that Dan wrote it all down. Other articles by Adam, Jeanne, Patrick and me. Download the April 1994 issue PDF and enjoy.
