I always wanted 4 kids. I grew up in a family whose entire existence was based on kids. My Mom was a nationally known poet, an author and a public speaker. Her poems were about the humorous side of raising kids! For example, one of her first poems was called Fast Burn! “If ever a morning should flower, with one fine harmonious hour, someone will spoil it by flushing the toilet while Daddy is taking a shower!” My Mom was so in to writing poetry that one morning she strapped me on the toilet and she forgot where she put me! After searching the neighborhood with the entire Cleveland Hts. Fire and Police Departments, one of the officers found me asleep on the toilet… So what did Mom do? She wrote a poem called “Scotty on the Potty” and read it to the whole world! Very embarrassing! I always felt that anyone who could make a career out of writing about toilets, let alone finding something to rhyme with one, had to be not only clever, but a positive thinker! My aunt, Mom’s, younger sister,  with a little help from Big Bird and Oscar the Grouch, started Channel 25 WVIZ TV educational television….and  I have a sister that started the local Therapeutic Riding Center. We have thirteen teachers in the family, a juvenile court judge… and way too many kids. So when I was 48 and my first wife changed her mind and decided that two cats were enough, I had to make a move! And when Michelle, along with her two sons, offered me her hand as a 50th birthday present, I thought my life was complete. But, be careful what you wish for!!! Chelle said she would give me one of “my own” if I promised her a daughter. I did, and after eating lots of brussel spouts, I came through… and she delivered! A Girl!!! and 10 minutes later, another boy!!! So in one year, at 51, I got what I always wanted… or so I thought! Having kids at this age does have it’s benefits! Financially, I think your more likely to be “settled in” money wise…  and there’s a good chance that my life insurance will cover their college. Emotionally, I worry about not waking up… but I hear from younger Dad’s that they worry about the opposite…. and, on another positive note, there’s a good chance I won’t have to give my daughter away! But physically, there are some draw backs. I had to get laser treatment so I could play ball with the boys… and my 6 year old daughter can run faster than me. All in all, I would say that having 4 kids about 10 years earlier in life might have had some advantages… but it wouldn’t have been a miracle! I still feel I’m the luckiest man alive! Hence, 2MNY2L8… it’s just the humorous side of having kids. Thanks, Moms! Scott Henry

June 6, 2008
Linda
