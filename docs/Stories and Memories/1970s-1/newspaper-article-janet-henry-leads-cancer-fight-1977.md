This article outlines how Janet agreed to become a cancer research study patient.  She was the first to receive immunization treatment as part of BCRP (Breast Cancer Research Project) that was a collaboration between University Hospitals, Cleveland Clinic and Metro General in 1977.

One quote from Janet “….I gave my body to science while I still was in it.  The answer to cancer is closer to us every minute”.
