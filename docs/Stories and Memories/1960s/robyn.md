This article about Aunt Janet and 2 year old Robyn appeared in the Cleveland Press on Valentine’s Day 1966.  It is a very cute story about how Janet received a horrible black eye from colliding with Robyn.  Of course, she wrote a poem about it.

By CHRISTINA WOLFE


NO MAKE-UP but a real-for-sure shiner covering Janet Henry's right eye and cheek bone is the result of collision with her two-year-old daughter Robyn.
Janet Henry, our house-wife poet in residence, dropped by last week look-ing almost incognito. Her dark, dark sunglasses were very chic, we all agreed on that—but hardly necessary on such a dark day.
Yes, said Janet, they certainly were necessary. She had a sizable shiner and was desperately try-ing to hide it from rumor-mongering eyes (not very successfully since it came down to her chin).
“People are calling me to say that they heard I had a ski accident (I don’t ski) or an auto accident. And my husband says, ‘Imagine what they ARE NOT telling us they heard,”  said Janet, who reports that she understands other women who have had a black eye.
"My children call me Batman, and my son wants me to wear an eye patch. They tell me not to go out and then they bring all their friends in to see me.
"Well, here is what hap-pened, and my neighbor witnessed it," she said tri-umphantly.
"It was one of those ghastly days when every-thing goes wrong. My car got stuck in the driveway and the neighbor's two-year-old locked herself in our bathroom and we couldn't get her out for 45 minutes. She just unlocked the door and wandered out, as I was unscrewing the last screw.
"Then to top it off, my two-year-old daughter, Robyn and I both tried to catch the dog and we collided. Her head hit my face," said Janet nodding in satisfaction. Robyn wasn't hurt. "I'm beginning to feel very defensive about all this," she added. However, Janet is still a poet and she couldn't resist.
Here I am in an up-to-date hairdo, And a skirt 21 inches high, And the latest of fashion in footwear, And a horrible looking black eye.
