This note from KC: “Betty and I visited Santa Fe Mike – we spent all our time visiting the INCREDIBLE buildings Mike has designed, renovated, made additions to. Private homes and business and retail/mall type centers. Amazing creative work!! I LOVED the adobe architecture! I LOVED Santa FE!!! Mike is a wonderful host and fantastic chef! He served home made pies, mushrooms he hand picked – over Elk he hunted and processed (AWESOME), vegies from his garden….. If you haven’t visited, don’t put this trip off…. It was such fun! Thanks cous!!!! :)”

November 8, 2007 Linda
