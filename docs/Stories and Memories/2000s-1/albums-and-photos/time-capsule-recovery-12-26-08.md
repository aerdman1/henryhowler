“Here are a few pictures documenting the recovery of the time capsule buried in 1992. After digging through frozen ground many holes to find the capsule you can tell the Eureka picture when pay dirt was hit. Unfortunately probably most of the time the capsule lay in a pool of collected water and everything was a soggy, stinky mess. Some things were distinguishable and Dottie, who by the way was the one who sorted through it all, brave soul, will display them outside tonight. New plans for the burial of this new time capsule to prevent the same unfortunate happening.” Jeanne Henry

**Diggers are: Chip, Todd, Patrick, Lori, Jim, Ross, Grace, Adam, Jeanne, Don and Dottie**
