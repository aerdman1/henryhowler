The first Findley Lake Musky Derby is history… George, Chip & Lil Bro’ Mike, Alex and Billy, George, Andy, Milo ans Dad… everyone caught Pike but “nosizetoem”. The “first-cast- 24″er” caught by the host (he had to catch two more 24″ers to prove he wasn’t cheating) won the money.   George kept the constant pressure on so there was no slacking up! Between the hot tub and the lake, Chip and Mike managed to get wet. Good people! Good food! Good boats, Great Poker! Good bass, …But no Musky! Here’s some pics… as you can see, regarding beds and boats, we had room for about 6 more! We’ll try again this fall…**Scott**
