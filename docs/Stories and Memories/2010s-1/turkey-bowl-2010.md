Thank goodness for the Thanksgiving Henry Family Turkey Bowl!

The game was played in the cold rain but the artificial turf at the Kenston Stadium made for a mud free morning.  This was the first year that we had a official ref…..Scott.  Made for some interesting “discussions” because as we all know,  Henry’s never argue with an official.

Kelly won the 2010 MVP award.  Congrats Kelly!  I included photos of her amazing run.

Lots of fun, no real lasting injuries with the exception of Andy’s big toe.  His toe surgery, in the evening, on Uncle Scott’s pool table, was a complete success thanks to the steady hands of Chip and Jim.  Ask Kim to see the complete video.
