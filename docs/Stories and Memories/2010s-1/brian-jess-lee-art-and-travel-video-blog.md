Digital Art Nomad is a business and lifestyle blog built for people interested in achieving a fun business travel lifestyle. Jess and I have built several business’ around our art allowing us to achieve freedom we never imagined was possible. Through hard work we have achieved the ability to live anywhere we want. We have created this YouTube channel especially for those who want to do the same. We have a lot of great information on the way and some really entertaining videos on the way so make sure to subscribe.

You can get more information at <Anchor label="www.digitalartnomad.com" target="_blank" href="http://www.digitalartnomad.com">[www.digitalartnomad.com](http://www.digitalartnomad.com)</Anchor>. Brian Lee digitalartnomad llc phone: 7779510654

Here are the episodes so far:

Episode 7 – Jan. 2017 In this Episode, we head back to the states to visit family as we prepare for more adventures abroad.

Episode 6  – Sept. 2016 In this episode we have some family visit, we show them around some or our favorite spots in Chiang Mai and then take them down south to the beautiful Koh Lanta island while we search for our new home.

Episode 5 – April 2016  In this Episode we show you a quick view of what it’s like to live as an independent artist in Chiang Mai. We show you some behind the scenes footage of digital art nomad lifestyle as we plan for our next adventure in southern Thailand.

Episode 4 – March 2016 In this Episode we travel into the mountains to Chiang Dao for the Shambala music festival.

Episode 3 – Feb. 2016  In this episode we continue the series of adventures that we started in the last episode through northern Thailand. This time we hike to the top of the largest mountain in Thailand (Doi Inthonan) , discover coffee plantations, learn to make coffee “jungle style”, jump off cliffs, discover new waterfalls, cat ice cream parlors, and a bunch more.

Episode 2 – Feb. 2016  In this Episode we embark on a series of adventures between Chiang Mai,Thailand and Chiang Rai, Thailand. We play with elephants, explore tea plantations, visit the legendary white temple, run through waterfalls, rock climb and a bunch more.

Episode 1 – Jan. 2016  In this first episode my wife jess and i get comfortable in our new home, Chiang Mai, Thailand . I share some interesting discoveries as we work on building our art businesses and push ourselves to get out of our home office and into the local culture to experience northern Thailand.
